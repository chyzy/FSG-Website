How it works

Install, connect, fly.

One small install on the PC, an app on the tablet — the boring parts happen by themselves.

Set up your PC — once

A small companion program on your flight-sim PC connects the apps to the simulator. The setup guide walks you through it; after that you can forget it exists.

The app finds your PC

Open the app on the tablet: it scans your network and lists your PC by name. Tap once to connect — the choice is remembered; from then on it connects on its own.

Fly

Every press and twist goes straight back into the sim. The first time you fly an aircraft, point at your PFD with a quick right Alt + click — the app guides you through it.

Features

Feels like a real instrument.

Every detail below is measured against the real thing in the simulator.

A bezel that feels right

Modeled on the simulator's own G1000: knurled knobs, softkeys with their little triangles, the full AFCS key block, key sounds and haptics. Touch targets are larger than the artwork, so fingers hit what eyes aim at.

Knobs you can actually turn

Turn with a circular gesture — 18° per detent, inner and outer rings, acceleration on the heading and course knobs when you spin fast. Tap for a single step, press the centre to push. Hold the COM swap key to punch in 121.5.

Full avionics control

All twelve softkeys, FMS knob and cursor, Direct-To, flight plan and procedures, NAV/COM tuning with volumes — and on the MFD: map range, joystick panning and every page group.

Built to stay current

Freshness beats smoothness on a flight instrument — and when your Wi-Fi has a bad day, the picture stays current rather than falling behind.

Nothing to babysit

Everything on the PC side runs itself: the G1000 is set up for you, and if something closes, it recovers on its own. If anything needs you, the app says so.

Nothing else to install

No FSUIPC, no plugins to hunt down, no browser tabs. An app on the tablet and a one-time setup on the PC — nothing more.

Aircraft

If it flies the G1000, it works.

FSG G1000 works with every aircraft in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 that carries the G1000 — from the Cessna 172 through the Diamonds and the Bonanza to the Cirrus. No per-aircraft profiles to download, nothing to configure: load the aircraft and fly.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need anything besides the app?

One thing: FSG SimBridge on your PC — the program the tablet talks to the simulator through. Installing it takes a couple of minutes, and the setup guide walks you through it.

Does it work with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 or other simulators?

Today it is built and verified for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 only.

Is there an iPhone/iPad version?

Android first — an iOS version is planned but not available yet.

Which aircraft are supported?

Every aircraft in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 that flies the G1000. The app works only with the G1000: load an aircraft with different avionics and it says “This aircraft has no G1000” instead of guessing.

Can I run the PFD and the MFD at the same time?

Yes. Both displays stay perfectly smooth, and commands from both work at the same time.

Does it work over the internet?

No — the tablet and the PC have to be on the same local network. That is what makes it feel instant.

The picture freezes or gets blurry in flight.

Almost always Wi-Fi contention: the simulator downloads scenery over the same radio. The fix list, in order of effectiveness, is in the network section of the setup guide — the first item is plugging the PC into Ethernet.
